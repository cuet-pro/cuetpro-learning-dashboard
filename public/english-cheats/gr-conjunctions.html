Types and Meaning

Coordinating, subordinating and what each does

Start here: Which conjunction? (follow the arrows)

Decide the relationship (contrast, reason, result), then pick the word.

1.1  Coordinating conjunctions (FANBOYS)

FOR

reason

He slept, for he was tired.

AND

addition

Tea and coffee

NOR

negative addition

Neither hot nor cold

BUT

contrast

Small but strong

OR

choice

Tea or coffee?

YET

surprising contrast

Poor yet happy

SO

result

It rained, so we left.

JOIN

equal parts

two clauses of the same rank

1.2  Subordinating conjunctions by meaning

TIME

when, while, before, after, until, since

Wait until I return.

REASON

because, since, as

Since you insist, I will go.

CONDITION

if, unless, provided that, as long as

Unless you hurry, you will be late.

CONTRAST

although, though, even though, whereas

Although it rained, we played.

PURPOSE

so that, in order that, lest

Study so that you pass.

COMPARISON

than, as...as

She is taller than I am.

M E M O R Y   H A C K     |     F A N B O Y S

For And Nor But Or Yet So. These seven join equal parts, usually with a comma before them in a long sentence.

2

Pairs and Special Rules

Correlatives and the tricky ones

2.1  Which pair?  (follow the arrows)

A pair is a team. Never mix the partners.

2.2  Rules that win marks

HARDLY / SCARCELY ... WHEN

not than

Hardly had he come when it rained.

NO SOONER ... THAN

not when

No sooner had he come than it rained.

UNLESS

means "if not", so no extra not

Unless you work, you will fail.

LEST

followed by should, no not

Walk fast lest you should be late.

THOUGH / ALTHOUGH

do not use but with them

Although he was tired, he worked.

AS IF / AS THOUGH

followed by past (unreal)

He talks as if he were a king.

SINCE / FOR

since = point, for = period

since 2010, for ten years

WHETHER ... OR NOT

use whether for two choices

I do not know whether he will come or not.

BOTH ... AND

not both...as well as

Both he and she came.

E X A M   T R A P S   W I T H

Although and but never together: Although he is poor, but he is honest → Although he is poor, he is honest.

 Unless already means "if not": Unless you do not work → Unless you work.

MEMORY HACK  |  HARDLY WHEN, NO SOONER THAN

Hardly / Scarcely pair with when. No sooner pairs with than. Both are followed by had + subject + V3.

WRONG

RIGHT

WHY

Although he is poor, but he is honest

Although he is poor, he is honest

no but with although

Hardly had he come than it rained

No sooner had he come than it rained

Unless you do not work, you will fail

Unless you work, you will fail

unless already negative

Either he or she is wrong

Walk fast lest you will be late

3.3  Mixed practice: choose the right conjunction

1.  (Although / Because) it was raining, we went out.

2.  He is poor (but / because) honest.

3.  Hardly had she left (than / when) the phone rang.

4.  No sooner did he arrive (than / when) it began to rain.

5.  (Unless / If) you hurry, you will miss the bus.

6.  Neither Ram (or / nor) Shyam knew the answer.

7.  Run fast (lest / so that) you miss the train.

8.  He did not come (because / because of) he was ill.

9.  You can have (either / neither) tea or coffee.

10.  I do not know (whether / if) he will come or not.

Answers: 1 Although, 2 but, 3 when, 4 than, 5 Unless, 6 nor, 7 lest, 8 because, 9 either, 10 whether.

6 0 - S E C O N D   R E C A P

FANBOYS join equal parts.

 Although / though drop the but.

 Hardly / scarcely ... when, no sooner ... than.

 Unless = if not (no second negative).

 Either ... or, neither ... nor, both ... and.

 Lest ... should.

 Because + clause, because of + noun.
