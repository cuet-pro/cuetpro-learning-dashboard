In / On / At

+ fixed pairs

A preposition links a noun to the rest of the sentence. CUET tests time and place, fixed word pairs and which preposition follows which word.

1

Time and Place

In, on, at and their cousins

Start here: In, on or at? (follow the arrows)

In is big, on is a surface, at is a point.

1.1  The three big ones

IN (TIME)

months, years, seasons, parts of day

in June, in 2020, in the morning

ON (TIME)

days and dates

on Sunday, on 5 May

AT (TIME)

clock time, festivals, night

at 6 pm, at night, at Diwali

IN (PLACE)

inside a space or town

in a room, in Delhi

ON (PLACE)

touching a surface

on the table, on the wall

AT (PLACE)

a point or a building as a place

at the gate, at the station

1.2  Other must-know prepositions

SINCE / FOR

since = point, for = period

since 2019, for two years

BY / UNTIL

by = deadline, until = continues till

Finish by 5. Wait until 5.

BETWEEN / AMONG

between two, among many

between you and me, among friends

BESIDE / BESIDES

beside = next to, besides = in addition

Besides Hindi, she knows French.

DURING / WHILE

during + noun, while + clause

during the war, while he slept

IN / WITHIN

within = inside a limit

Return within a week.

M E M O R Y   H A C K     |     I N - O N - A T   F U N N E L

Time goes from big to small: in (year, month) → on (day, date) → at (clock time). In 2024, on 5 May, at 6 pm.

2

Fixed Pairs

Words that always come with one preposition

2.1  Which preposition after this word?  (follow the arrows)

Most prepositions here are fixed. Learn them as pairs.

2.2  Pairs you must know

ADJECTIVE + OF

afraid, proud, jealous, fond, aware, capable, short

proud of you, fond of music

ADJECTIVE + TO

known, kind, married, similar, superior, junior

married to, superior to, prefer A to B

ADJECTIVE + IN / AT / WITH

interested in, good at, weak in, angry with, satisfied with

good at maths, interested in art

VERB + ON

depend, rely, insist, focus, congratulate on

rely on him, congratulate her on her win

VERB + FOR

wait, ask, search, apologise, look for

wait for him, search for a pen

VERB + FROM / WITH

suffer from, differ from, agree with, deal with

suffer from fever, deal with it

E X A M   T R A P S   W I T H

Prefer takes to, never than: I prefer tea than coffee → I prefer tea to coffee.

 Discuss takes no preposition: discuss about it → discuss it.

 Reach takes none: reach to Delhi → reach Delhi.

M E M O R Y   H A C K     |     N O   P R E P O S I T I O N   G A N G

Verbs that take no preposition: discuss, reach, enter, attend, resemble, order, request. She resembles her mother. He entered the room.

3.3  Mixed practice: fill in the preposition

1.  The meeting is ___ Monday.

2.  He was born ___ 1999.

3.  She is fond ___ music.

4.  I am interested ___ painting.

5.  They are waiting ___ the bus.

6.  He is junior ___ me.

7.  Please divide the sweets ___ the two boys.

8.  The sweets are shared ___ the three of us.

9.  She is angry ___ her brother.

10.  He apologised ___ being late.

Answers: 1 on, 2 in, 3 of, 4 in, 5 for, 6 to, 7 between, 8 among, 9 with, 10 for.
