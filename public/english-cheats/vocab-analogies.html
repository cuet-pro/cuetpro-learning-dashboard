A : B :: C : ?

+ 10 relationships

An analogy asks you to find the same relationship in a second pair. Never look at the words alone. Name the relationship in one sentence, then test each option with it.

Make a sentence for the first pair, then reuse it. "A calf is the young one of a cow" → "A kitten is the young one of a cat."

2

Tricks and Elimination

Test the options fast

2.1  Which option?  (follow the arrows)

The bridge sentence must work in both pairs word for word.

2.2  Rules that win marks

KEEP THE ORDER

whole : part stays whole : part

car : wheel / house : door

SAME PART OF SPEECH

noun : noun, verb : verb

run : walk needs shout : whisper

DEGREE

weak → strong must match

like : love / warm : hot

TOOL : USE

tool and its job

knife : cut / pen : write

SYMBOL : MEANING

sign and idea

dove : peace

PLACE : PERSON

work place

court : judge / school : teacher

STUDY : SUBJECT

science and what it studies

botany : plants

FEAR WORDS

phobia : fear of

claustrophobia : closed spaces

E X A M   T R A P S   W I T H

Order trap: if the first pair is part : whole, the second must also be part : whole.

 Two close options: refine your bridge sentence ("a tool used to cut", not "related to") and re-test.

 Not by topic: "doctor : hospital" is worker : workplace, not just "medical".

M E M O R Y   H A C K     |     B R I D G E   B O T H   W A Y S

If your bridge sentence works for the first pair but only loosely for the option, reject it. Only an exact reuse wins.
