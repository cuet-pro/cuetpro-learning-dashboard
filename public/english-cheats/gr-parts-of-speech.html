Every word has a job. A word's part of speech depends on how it is used, not on its spelling. CUET asks you to identify the part of speech of an underlined word.

The Job Test

Same word, different part of speech

2.1  One word, many jobs  (follow the arrows)

Position in the sentence shows the part of speech.

2.2  Rules that win marks

NOUN TEST

can follow the / a

the book, a hope

VERB TEST

changes with tense

walk / walked / will walk

ADJECTIVE TEST

can follow very or be

very tall, She is kind.

ADVERB TEST

often ends -ly, answers how / when / where

quickly, soon, there

PREPOSITION TEST

comes before a noun

in the room, at noon

CONJUNCTION TEST

joins two parts

He came but left.

SAME WORD, TWO JOBS

after: preposition or conjunction

After lunch (prep). After he left (conj).

FAST

adjective or adverb

a fast train (adj); runs fast (adv)

ROUND

noun, verb, adjective, adverb, preposition

a round table (adj)

E X A M   T R A P S   W I T H

Do not judge by spelling: "early" is an adverb — it can also be an adjective (the early bird).

 Words ending in -ly are not always adverbs: friendly, lovely, lonely are adjectives.

3.3  Mixed practice: name the part of speech

1.  The lion roared. (noun / verb)

2.  She quickly left. (adjective / adverb)

3.  It is a beautiful day. (adjective / noun)

4.  He is in the room. (preposition / adverb)

5.  Tea and coffee are hot. (conjunction / pronoun)

6.  Wow! That is amazing. (interjection / noun)

7.  She is kind. (adjective / verb)

8.  He runs fast. (noun / verb)

9.  They won. (pronoun / noun)

10.  He works hard. (adjective / adverb)

Answers: 1 noun, 2 adverb, 3 adjective, 4 preposition, 5 conjunction, 6 interjection, 7 adjective, 8 verb, 9 pronoun, 10 adverb.

Eight parts: noun, pronoun, verb, adjective, adverb, preposition, conjunction, interjection.

 Job decides the part, not spelling.

 Adjective before a noun; adverb near the verb.

 -ly does not always mean adverb (lovely, friendly).

 After / before can be preposition or conjunction.
