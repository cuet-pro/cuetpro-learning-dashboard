Same Meaning

+ context clues

A synonym is a word with the same or nearly the same meaning. CUET gives a word (often in a sentence) and asks for the closest meaning. Context decides, not the dictionary alone.

Guess the Meaning

How to work out an unknown word

Start here: Guess the meaning from context (follow the arrows)

Clue words, then roots, then tone.

1.1  Context clues

CONTRAST

but, however, although

He was frugal, but his brother was lavish.

EXAMPLE

such as, like, for example

Rare animals, like the snow leopard.

RESTATEMENT

that is, or, in other words

Candid, or frank.

CAUSE / EFFECT

because, so

Because he was ecstatic, he danced.

TONE

positive or negative sentence

praise → positive synonym

ROOTS

break the word into parts

benevolent, malevolent

M E M O R Y   H A C K     |     C L U E - W O R D   H U N T

Look for but, like, such as, that is, because. They point straight to the meaning of the unknown word.

E X A M   T R A P S   W I T H

One word, two meanings: fair = just OR pale; bear = tolerate OR animal. Use the sentence.

 Opposite trap: an option may be an antonym; avoid it.

 Strength: "furious" is stronger than "annoyed".

3.3  Mixed practice: choose the closest synonym

1.  Frugal: (wasteful / thrifty / rich / lazy)

2.  Candid: (frank / secret / rude / shy)

3.  Arduous: (easy / difficult / boring / long)

4.  Elated: (joyful / angry / tired / lost)

5.  Colossal: (tiny / huge / quiet / old)

6.  Valiant: (timid / brave / weak / wise)

7.  Irate: (calm / angry / happy / sleepy)

8.  Vital: (minor / essential / hidden / odd)

9.  Meagre: (scanty / plentiful / rich / bright)

10.  Obsolete: (outdated / modern / useful / fresh)

Answers: 1 thrifty, 2 frank, 3 difficult, 4 joyful, 5 huge, 6 brave, 7 angry, 8 essential, 9 scanty, 10 outdated.
