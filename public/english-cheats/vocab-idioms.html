Not Literal

+ 40 must-knows

An idiom is a fixed phrase whose meaning is not the sum of its words. "Kick the bucket" is not about a bucket. CUET asks you to choose the meaning of the idiom, so learn idioms in groups.

1.1  Idioms by theme

SUCCESS

a feather in one's cap = an achievement

hit the jackpot = win big

FAILURE

bite the dust = fail or fall

fall flat = fail completely

ANGER

hit the roof = get very angry

see red = lose temper

SECRETS

spill the beans = reveal a secret

let the cat out of the bag

HARD WORK

burn the midnight oil = study late

go the extra mile

TIME / LUCK

once in a blue moon = rarely

a blessing in disguise

1.2  Idioms with animals and body parts

ANIMALS

a wild goose chase = a useless search

white elephant = costly useless thing

BODY PARTS

cost an arm and a leg = very expensive

keep an eye on = watch

COLOURS

green with envy = jealous

in the red = in debt

Literal trap: "raining cats and dogs" is heavy rain, not animals falling.

 Similar-looking idioms: let the cat out of the bag (reveal secret) vs curiosity killed the cat (curiosity is risky).

 Fixed form: do not change words; "break the ice" is not "break the snow".

3.3  Mixed practice: choose the meaning

1.  A piece of cake: (a dessert / very easy / cheap / sweet)

2.  To bite the bullet: (face pain bravely / shoot / eat quickly / cheat)

3.  Under the weather: (outdoors / slightly ill / cold / happy)

4.  To burn the midnight oil: (waste fuel / study late / cook / sleep late)

5.  A white elephant: (rare animal / costly useless thing / big pet / lucky charm)

6.  To hit the nail on the head: (be exactly right / hurt oneself / build / argue)

7.  Once in a blue moon: (rarely / at night / daily / often)

8.  To let the cat out of the bag: (reveal a secret / lose a pet / start a fight / run away)

9.  A blessing in disguise: (hidden good fortune / a prayer / bad luck / a mask)

10.  To cry over spilt milk: (regret the past / waste milk / be sad / cook)

Answers: 1 very easy, 2 face pain bravely, 3 slightly ill, 4 study late, 5 costly useless thing, 6 be exactly right, 7 rarely, 8 reveal a secret, 9 hidden good fortune, 10 regret the past.
