A / An / The

+ zero article

Articles are the tiny words a, an, the. They look easy but they decide many CUET marks. Think about sound, first mention and specific or general.

1

A, An and The

Which article goes before which noun

Start here: Which article? (follow the arrows)

Ask: "Which one?" If the listener knows, use the.

1.1  A or an? Listen to the sound, not the spelling

AN + VOWEL SOUND

a, e, i, o, u sound

an apple, an hour, an honest man

A + CONSONANT SOUND

even if spelled with a vowel

a university, a European, a one-rupee note

AN + SILENT H

hour, honest, honour, heir

an heir, an honour

1.2  When to use THE

ALREADY MENTIONED

second mention

I met a boy. The boy was kind.

UNIQUE THINGS

only one exists

the sun, the moon, the sky

SUPERLATIVES

the best, the first

She is the tallest girl.

RIVERS, SEAS, OCEANS

also mountain ranges

the Ganga, the Himalayas

NEWSPAPERS, HOLY BOOKS

special names

the Hindu, the Gita

THE + ADJECTIVE

a whole group

the rich, the poor

M E M O R Y   H A C K     |     S O U N D   N O T   S P E L L I N G

Say the word aloud. an hour (silent h, vowel sound) but a university (starts with a "yoo" sound). The first sound decides a or an.

2

Rules and Omission

When to skip the article and special patterns

2.1  Do I need an article here?  (follow the arrows)

Place names used for their purpose take no article.

2.2  Rules that win marks

PROPER NOUNS

no article

India is large. Delhi is a city.

LANGUAGES, SUBJECTS

no article

She speaks French. I like Science.

MEALS

no article, unless specific

We had lunch. We had a good lunch.

SUCH A / WHAT A / QUITE A

a goes after such, what, quite

Such a nice day! What a pity!

HALF, DOUBLE, ALL, BOTH

article comes AFTER these

Half the cake, both the boys

MANY A, A FEW, A LITTLE

meaning of few: a few = some, few = hardly any

I have a few friends.

E X A M   T R A P S   W I T H

Never put an article before plural nouns used in general: The dogs are loyal (about all dogs) → Dogs are loyal.

 Never use a before uncountable nouns: an advice → a piece of advice.

M E M O R Y   H A C K     |     N A M E S   N E E D   N O   N A M E - T A G

Names of people, countries, cities, languages and subjects wear no article (India, Hindi, Maths). Rivers, seas, ranges wear the (the Ganga, the Alps).

3.3  Mixed practice: fill in a / an / the / no article

1.  He is ___ honest man.

2.  She plays ___ guitar well.

3.  I saw ___ owl. ___ owl was huge.

4.  ___ Himalayas are in the north.

5.  He is ___ one-eyed man.

6.  She goes to ___ church every Sunday.

7.  ___ sun rises in the east.

8.  It was ___ unusual event.

9.  Ravi is ___ best player in the team.

10.  ___ gold is a precious metal.

Answers: 1 an, 2 the (musical instrument), 3 an / The, 4 The, 5 a, 6 (no article), 7 The, 8 an, 9 the, 10 (no article).

6 0 - S E C O N D   R E C A P

Sound decides a / an: an hour, a university.

 The = known, unique, superlative, rivers, ranges.

 No article: names, languages, subjects, meals, general plurals.

 Musical instruments take the: play the guitar.

 Such a / what a: article after such / what.

 Half / both / all + the.
