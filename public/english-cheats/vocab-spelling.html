Spelling Rules

Patterns for ie / ei, doubling and endings

Start here: i before e? (follow the arrows)

I before E, except after C, or when sounded as "ay".

1.1  Rules at a glance

DOUBLING

one-syllable CVC words double the last letter

run → running, sit → sitting

DROP THE E

before -ing

make → making, write → writing

KEEP THE E

before -ment, -ful, -ly

manage → management, hope → hopeful

Y TO I

consonant + y

happy → happiness, carry → carried

PLURALS

-s, -es, -ies

boxes, babies, heroes

-FUL HAS ONE L

not -full

careful, useful, beautiful

M E M O R Y   H A C K     |     I   B E F O R E   E

I before E, except after C. belief, achieve / receive, ceiling. And "ay" sound: weigh, neighbour.

2.2  30 words to memorise

A TO C

accommodate, achievement, address, beginning, business, calendar, committee, conscience, correspondence

D TO M

definitely, discipline, embarrass, environment, exaggerate, February, government, guarantee, immediately, maintenance

N TO Z

necessary, occasion, occurrence, possession, privilege, receive, recommend, separate, successful, restaurant, unnecessary, until

E X A M   T R A P S   W I T H

Twin letters: accomodate → accommodate; occurence → occurrence.

 Silent letters: goverment → government; Febuary → February.

 Wrong vowels: seperate → separate.

M E M O R Y   H A C K     |     S E P A R A T E   H A S   " A   R A T "

separate: "there is a rat in sep-a-rate". And necessary: one Collar, two Socks (1 c, 2 s).
