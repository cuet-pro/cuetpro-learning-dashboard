Poems & Essays

+ tone & devices

A literary passage uses figurative language, tone and theme: essays, speeches, poems, reflections. Questions ask about tone, devices, theme and meaning of lines. Read for what the writer feels, not only what is said.

2.3  Worked mini-example

Passage: "Grandmother's kitchen smelled of cardamom and old stories. I would give anything to sit there once more."

Q: WHAT IS THE TONE?

Tone. "give anything to sit there once more" = nostalgic.

Q: WHAT DOES "OLD STORIES" ADD?

Device. It gives the smell a feeling of memory and warmth.

E X A M   T R A P S   W I T H

Literal reading of a metaphor: "Time is a thief" does not mean time steals objects.

 Wrong tone: "sad" is not the same as "bitter" or "nostalgic".

 Over-reading: stay within the text.

M E M O R Y   H A C K     |     I M A G E   →   I D E A

Turn every image into an idea. Sun dipping into the sea = the day ending. Locked door = no way forward.
