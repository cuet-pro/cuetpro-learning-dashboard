ACTIVE & PASSIVE VOICE

CUET UG English Grammar  |  Quick-recap notes with flowcharts

Doer / Receiver

+ all tenses

In active voice the subject does the action; in passive voice the subject receives it. The passive is built as be + V3. CUET asks you to convert or to choose the correct passive.

1

Making the Passive

The recipe and the be-form for every tense

Start here: Convert to passive (follow the arrows)

Only verbs with an object (transitive) can become passive.

1.1  The passive for each tense

PRESENT SIMPLE

is / am / are + V3

Letters are written daily.

PRESENT CONTINUOUS

is / am / are being + V3

A letter is being written.

PRESENT PERFECT

has / have been + V3

A letter has been written.

PAST SIMPLE

was / were + V3

A letter was written.

PAST CONTINUOUS

was / were being + V3

A letter was being written.

PAST PERFECT

had been + V3

A letter had been written.

FUTURE SIMPLE

will be + V3

A letter will be written.

MODALS

modal + be + V3

A letter can be written.

FUTURE PERFECT

will have been + V3

A letter will have been written.

M E M O R Y   H A C K     |     3   S T E P S

1. Object becomes subject. 2. Verb becomes be + V3 (be in the same tense). 3. Subject becomes by + object (or drops). Ram ate a mango → A mango was eaten by Ram.

2

Special Passives

Questions, commands and tricky verbs

2.1  Which type of sentence?  (follow the arrows)

The sentence type decides how you start the passive.

2.2  Rules that win marks

IMPERATIVE

Let + object + be + V3

Close the window. → Let the window be closed.

REQUEST

You are requested to ...

Please sit. → You are requested to sit.

INTERROGATIVE

Do / does / did disappears

Did she write it? → Was it written by her?

WH- QUESTIONS

Wh- word stays first

Who wrote it? → By whom was it written?

VERBS WITH 2 OBJECTS

either object can be the subject

He gave me a book. → I was given a book.

INTRANSITIVE VERBS

no passive

He was slept.

STATIVE VERBS

have, resemble, lack: no passive

A car is had by him.

INFINITIVE

to be + V3

He wants to be told.

GERUND

being + V3

I like being praised.

E X A M   T R A P S   W I T H

Never drop "be": The letter written by her → The letter was written by her.

 V3 after be: The cake was eat → was eaten.

 Wh- questions: "Who" becomes "by whom".

M E M O R Y   H A C K     |     P A S S I V E   =   B E   +   V 3

No matter the tense, passive = be-form + V3. Only the be-form changes: is, was, will be, has been, had been, is being, was being.

3.3  Mixed practice: convert to passive

1.  She sings a song. →  A song ___ by her.

2.  They are building a bridge. →  A bridge ___ built.

3.  He has finished the work. →  The work ___ finished.

4.  Ram will help me. →  I ___ helped by Ram.

5.  Who broke the glass? →  By whom ___ the glass broken?

6.  Open the door. →  Let the door ___ opened.

7.  The cook was preparing dinner. →  Dinner ___ prepared.

8.  Did she win the prize? →  ___ the prize won by her?

9.  They gave him a prize. →  He ___ given a prize.

10.  We must obey rules. →  Rules must ___ obeyed.

Answers: 1 is sung, 2 is being, 3 has been, 4 will be, 5 was, 6 be, 7 was being, 8 Was, 9 was, 10 be.

6 0 - S E C O N D   R E C A P

Passive = be + V3 in the same tense as the active verb.

 Object becomes subject, subject becomes by + object.

 Continuous: is / was being + V3.

 Perfect: has / have / had been + V3.

 Command: let + be + V3.

 No passive for intransitive or stative verbs.
