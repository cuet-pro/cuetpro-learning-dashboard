E X A M   T R A P S   W I T H

Faux pas is a blunder, not a type of food.

 Rendezvous is a meeting, not a rule.

 Bona fide means genuine, not a legal trick: a bona fide buyer really intends to buy.

M E M O R Y   H A C K     |     F A U X   =   F A L S E

faux pas = "false step" = a blunder. faux fur = fake fur. faux always means fake.

3.3  Mixed practice: choose the meaning

1.  bona fide: (genuine / fake / old / late)

2.  ad hoc: (permanent / for this purpose / free / large)

3.  vice versa: (the other way round / in turn / next time / never)

4.  status quo: (present state / future plan / past event / loss)

5.  faux pas: (blunder / dish / dance / song)

6.  deja vu: (a new idea / feeling of having seen before / a dream / a lie)

7.  en route: (on the way / at home / in debt / at ease)

8.  alma mater: (old school or college / foreign aunt / loud noise / soft toy)

9.  rendezvous: (a meeting / a rule / a meal / a trade)

10.  et cetera: (and so on / but not / at last / only)

Answers: 1 genuine, 2 for this purpose, 3 the other way round, 4 present state, 5 blunder, 6 feeling of having seen before, 7 on the way, 8 old school or college, 9 a meeting, 10 and so on.
