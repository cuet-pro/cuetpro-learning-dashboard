An antonym is a word with the opposite meaning. CUET gives a word and four options. You pick the closest opposite in the context given.

not

2.2  Rules that win marks

SAME PART OF SPEECH

noun ↔ noun

success ↔ failure

STRENGTH MATTERS

extreme ↔ extreme

ecstatic ↔ miserable

CONTEXT

meaning can change by sentence

fair (just) ↔ unjust, fair (pale) ↔ dark

WATCH NEAR-PAIRS

sound-alikes confuse

ascend ↔ descend

GRADABLE

big ↔ small, not tiny

keep the same degree

ROOTS

bene = good, mal = bad

benefit ↔ harm

E X A M   T R A P S   W I T H

Synonym trap: one option is a synonym of the given word; never pick it.

 Partial opposites: "rarely" is a weaker opposite of "often" than "never".

 Look-alikes: stationary (still) and stationery (paper) are different words.

M E M O R Y   H A C K     |     R O O T   S H O R T C U T S

bene = good, mal = bad. pro = for, anti = against. ex = out, in = in. Know a root and you often know the opposite.

3.3  Mixed practice: choose the opposite

1.  Generous: (kind / stingy / rich / noble)

2.  Ancient: (modern / old / ruined / historic)

3.  Abundant: (plenty / scarce / large / rich)

4.  Optimist: (dreamer / pessimist / realist / fool)

5.  Expand: (grow / contract / enlarge / extend)

6.  Victory: (win / defeat / battle / glory)

7.  Brave: (bold / timid / mighty / loud)

8.  Accept: (take / reject / praise / allow)

9.  Rapid: (swift / slow / quick / light)

10.  Fertile: (rich / barren / green / soft)

Answers: 1 stingy, 2 modern, 3 scarce, 4 pessimist, 5 contract, 6 defeat, 7 timid, 8 reject, 9 slow, 10 barren.
