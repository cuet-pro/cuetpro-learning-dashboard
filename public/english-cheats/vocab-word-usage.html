1

Confusing Pairs

Look-alike and sound-alike words

Start here: Which of the two? (follow the arrows)

Decide the part of speech first; it usually settles the pair.

1.1  Pairs with a one-line job

AFFECT / EFFECT

affect = verb (to influence), effect = noun (result)

The rain affected the crop. The effect was bad.

ACCEPT / EXCEPT

accept = receive, except = but

I accept the gift. Everyone except him came.

PRINCIPAL / PRINCIPLE

principal = head, principle = rule

The principal has high principles.

STATIONARY / STATIONERY

still / paper goods

The car is stationary. Buy stationery.

LOOSE / LOSE

not tight / fail to keep

loose shirt, lose a game

COMPLEMENT / COMPLIMENT

complete / praise

a complement of colours, a compliment

1.2  More pairs

ADVICE / ADVISE

noun / verb

Take my advice. I advise you.

BREATH / BREATHE

noun / verb

Take a breath. Breathe slowly.

THEIR / THERE / THEY'RE

owns / place / they are

Their car is there.

ITS / IT'S

belongs to it / it is

It's a dog; its tail wags.

THAN / THEN

compare / time

taller than; then he left

QUIET / QUITE

silent / very

quiet room; quite good

M E M O R Y   H A C K     |     A   F O R   A C T I O N ,   E   F O R   E N D

Affect = Action (verb). Effect = End result (noun). Smoking affects health; the effect is illness.

Using Words Correctly

Test the word in context

2.1  Right word or wrong word?  (follow the arrows)

Test the word by reading it into the sentence.

2.2  Rules that win marks

MAKE / DO

make = create, do = perform

make a cake, do homework

SAY / TELL

say = words, tell = person

say hello, tell me

RISE / RAISE

rise = go up, raise = lift something

The sun rises. Raise your hand.

LAY / LIE

lay = put, lie = recline

Lay the book. Lie down.

FEWER / LESS

countable / uncountable

fewer pens, less water

BESIDE / BESIDES

next to / in addition

beside the door, besides Hindi

BORROW / LEND

take / give

borrow a pen, lend a pen

HEAR / LISTEN

ears / attention

hear a noise, listen to music

SEE / LOOK / WATCH

notice / direct / follow

watch a film

E X A M   T R A P S   W I T H

Part of speech: advise (verb) vs advice (noun).

 Collocations: we make a mistake and do homework (not make homework).

 Its / it's: apostrophe means "it is".

M E M O R Y   H A C K     |     M A K E   =   C R E A T E

make something new (a cake, a plan, a mistake); do a task or a duty (homework, a favour, work).

3.3  Mixed practice: pick the correct word

1.  The medicine had no (affect / effect).

2.  I cannot (accept / except) this gift.

3.  Take my (advice / advise).

4.  The (principal / principle) is strict.

5.  (Its / It's) a sunny day.

6.  He is older (than / then) me.

7.  My shoes are too (lose / loose).

8.  The traffic was (stationary / stationery).

9.  She is (quiet / quite) tall.

10.  Everybody (accept / except) Ravi came.

Answers: 1 effect, 2 accept, 3 advice, 4 principal, 5 It's, 6 than, 7 loose, 8 stationary, 9 quite, 10 except.

6 0 - S E C O N D   R E C A P

Affect = verb, effect = noun.

 Accept = receive, except = but.

 Advice = noun, advise = verb.

 Principal = head, principle = rule.

 It's = it is, its = of it.

 Than = compare, then = time.

 Make = create, do = perform.
