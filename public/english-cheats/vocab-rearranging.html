Jumbled Sentences

+ 5 clue types

In rearranging, a paragraph or sentence is broken into jumbled parts (P, Q, R, S). You put them in the right order. The trick is find the opening, then follow the linking clues.

1

Find the Opening

Start with the right sentence

Start here: Which part starts? (follow the arrows)

The opener is general and self-sufficient. A pronoun or linker cannot open.

1.1  Opening and ending clues

THE OPENER

introduces the person or topic fully

Ravi is a student.

NOT AN OPENER

starts with he, she, it, this, these, however, but

He studies hard.

THE CLOSER

summarises, concludes, uses so / thus

So he passed.

1.2  Linking words that show order

ADDITION

also, besides, moreover, in addition

CONTRAST

but, however, yet, on the other hand

RESULT

so, therefore, thus, as a result

M E M O R Y   H A C K     |     O P E N E R - P R O N O U N - L I N K E R

1. Find the opener (full noun, no pronoun). 2. Match each pronoun to its noun. 3. Follow the linkers. 4. Put the closer last.

2

Follow the Clues

Pronouns, linkers and logic

2.1  Which clue to use?  (follow the arrows)

Pronoun, contrast and result clues fix the order.

2.2  Rules that win marks

NOUN BEFORE PRONOUN

name first, then he / she

Ravi came. He sat.

GENERAL BEFORE SPECIFIC

broad idea, then detail

Plants need water. Roots absorb it.

CAUSE BEFORE EFFECT

reason first

It rained. So we stayed.

TIME ORDER

first, next, then, finally

First ... Finally ...

QUESTION THEN ANSWER

a question comes first

Why do we sleep? To rest.

DEFINITE ARTICLE

a first mention, the after

A boy came. The boy sat.

E X A M   T R A P S   W I T H

Pronoun trap: a part beginning with "he" cannot be first.

 Linker trap: "However" must follow an idea it contrasts.

 Article trap: "A boy" first, "the boy" later.

M E M O R Y   H A C K     |     A   T H E N   T H E

First mention takes a / an; the second mention takes the. A king ruled. The king was kind.

3.3  Mixed practice: order the parts

1.  P: He studied hard.  Q: Ravi is a student.  R: So he passed.  S: He wanted to succeed.  →  order?

2.  P: It rained.  Q: We stayed home.  R: So we watched TV.  →  order?

3.  P: A boy came.  Q: The boy sat.  R: He smiled.  →  order?

4.  P: However, he failed.  Q: He worked hard.  R: Ravi is a student.  →  order?

5.  P: First, mix the flour.  Q: Then add water.  R: Finally, bake it.  →  order?

6.  P: Water is vital.  Q: It supports life.  R: We must save it.  →  order?

7.  P: She was ill.  Q: So she rested.  R: Meena is a girl.  →  order?

8.  P: He won.  Q: He trained daily.  R: Arjun is an athlete.  →  order?

9.  P: This helps health.  Q: Exercise is good.  R: Walk daily.  →  order?

10.  P: Rahul missed the bus.  Q: He reached late.  R: Rahul left home late.  →  order?
