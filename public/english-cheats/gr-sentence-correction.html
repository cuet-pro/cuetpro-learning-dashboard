The 4-Step Method

How to attack every question

Start here: Attack plan (follow the arrows)

Read the whole sentence, find the error type, then test each option.

1.1  The four steps

STEP 1  READ

Read the full sentence once for meaning, not just the underlined part.

STEP 2  SPOT

Run the checklist: S-V, tense, pronoun, parallel structure, modifier.

STEP 3  ELIMINATE

Cross out options that repeat the error or add a new one.

STEP 4  RE-READ

Put your choice into the sentence and read it again aloud in your head.

1.2  What is usually tested

AGREEMENT

subject and verb

The boys play (not plays)

TENSE

time words

He left yesterday

PARALLEL STRUCTURE

same form in a list

She likes singing, dancing and painting

PRONOUN

case and reference

She and I went

MODIFIER

place next to the noun

Walking home, I met a friend

REDUNDANCY

no repeated meaning

return (not return back)

M E M O R Y   H A C K     |     O P T I O N   A   =   S A M E

Option A usually repeats the original. Choose it only when you have checked all the error types and found none.

2.2  Rules that win marks

PARALLELISM

list items in the same form

He likes to read, to write and to draw.

DANGLING MODIFIER

the -ing doer must be the subject

Walking home, a dog bit me → Walking home, I was bitten by a dog.

REDUNDANCY

avoid double meaning

return back, repeat again, free gift

WORDINESS

prefer the short phrase

due to the fact that → because

COMPARISON

compare like with like

The climate of Delhi is hotter than that of Shimla.

DOUBLE NEGATIVE

one negative only

I do not know nothing → I know nothing.

SUBJUNCTIVE

if I were

If I were you, I would go.

PRONOUN REFERENCE

be clear who "it" is

When Ram met Mohan, he smiled (who?)

TENSE SEQUENCE

keep the same time

He said he would come.

E X A M   T R A P S   W I T H

Dangling modifiers: the noun after the comma must be the one doing the -ing action.

 Wrong comparison: Delhi's climate is hotter than Shimla → than that of Shimla.

 Redundancy is usually wrong in an option; shorter is safer.

M E M O R Y   H A C K     |     S H O R T   A N D   C L E A R   W I N S

When two options are both grammatical, choose the shorter, clearer one. Examiners reward clean sentences.

3.3  Mixed practice: choose the best replacement

1.  The boys ___ (is / are) playing outside.

2.  She likes reading, writing and ___ (to paint / painting).

3.  If I ___ (was / were) rich, I would help them.

4.  Please ___ (return back / return) the book.

5.  The weather of Delhi is hotter than ___ (Shimla / that of Shimla).

6.  ___ (Running to the station / When he ran to the station), the bag fell.

7.  The reason he left is ___ (because / that) he was angry.

8.  He ___ (did not come / did not came) yesterday.

9.  Each of the students ___ (has / have) a book.

10.  Hardly had she left ___ (when / than) it rained.

Answers: 1 are, 2 painting, 3 were, 4 return, 5 that of Shimla, 6 When he ran to the station, 7 that, 8 did not come, 9 has, 10 when.
