Verb + Particle

+ 40 must-knows

A phrasal verb is a verb + a small word (up, down, off, out, in) that makes a new meaning. "Give up" does not mean "give". CUET asks you to pick the meaning or the correct particle.

MEMORY HACK  |  VERB + PARTICLE = NEW WORD

Treat a phrasal verb as one new word. give up = quit. give in = surrender. give away = donate. The particle changes the whole meaning.

2

Meaning and Use

Same verb, different meaning

2.1  Which particle?  (follow the arrows)

Particles carry meaning: up (finish), off (stop), out (discover).

2.2  Rules that win marks

LOOK

look after = care, look for = search, look into = investigate, look up to = respect

TAKE

take off = leave the ground, take after = resemble, take up = begin a hobby

PUT

put off = postpone, put on = wear, put up with = tolerate

GET

get along = be friendly, get over = recover, get away = escape

BREAK

break down = stop working, break up = end, break out = start suddenly

RUN

run into = meet by chance, run out of = finish, run over = knock down

MAKE

make up = invent, make out = understand, make for = go towards

COME

come across = find by chance, come up with = invent, come by = obtain

GO

go on = continue, go through = suffer, go off = explode

E X A M   T R A P S   W I T H

Look after (care) is not look for (search). Put off (postpone) is not put on (wear).

 Separable: turn off the light = turn it off (never turn off it).

 Put up with has three parts and cannot be split.

M E M O R Y   H A C K     |     L O O K - A F T E R ,   L O O K - F O R

look AFTER a child (care, follow behind). look FOR a lost thing (search). look INTO a crime (investigate).

3.3  Mixed practice: choose the right phrasal verb

1.  The match was (called off / called on) due to rain.

2.  She (takes after / takes up) her mother.

3.  Please (look after / look for) my cat.

4.  He decided to (give up / give in) smoking.

5.  We have (run out of / run into) sugar.

6.  They had to (put off / put on) the meeting.

7.  Can you (look for / look after) my keys?

8.  I cannot (put up with / put out) this noise.

9.  He (got over / got on) his illness quickly.

10.  The car (broke down / broke up) on the highway.

Answers: 1 called off, 2 takes after, 3 look after, 4 give up, 5 run out of, 6 put off, 7 look for, 8 put up with, 9 got over, 10 broke down.
