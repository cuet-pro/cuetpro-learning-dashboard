Spot the Mistake

In error detection you read four parts of a sentence and find the part with the mistake. Scan for the usual suspects in a fixed order and you will be fast and accurate.

Spot It Fast

Signals and patterns

2.1  If you see X, check Y  (follow the arrows)

A signal word tells you which rule is being tested.

2.2  Signal → Check

TWO THINGS COMPARED

check comparative

She is taller than me (not more taller)

THE + ADJECTIVE

plural meaning

The poor are (not is)

EACH / EVERY

singular verb and pronoun

Each boy has his pen

UNCOUNTABLE

furniture, advice, news: no plural

The furniture is new

IF / WHEN CLAUSE

present, not will

If it rains, we stay

PAST HABIT

used to + V1

He used to play cricket

PREFER

to not than

I prefer tea to coffee

LOOK, FEEL, TASTE

adjective follows

She looks happy, not happily

NO ERROR

if all checks pass, choose No error

E X A M   T R A P S   W I T H

Do not hunt only for big mistakes. Many errors are tiny: a / an, s / es, to / for.

 Read the full sentence once before you underline a part.

 If you are 50% sure of an error, go with it; "No error" is only the answer about once in five.

M E M O R Y   H A C K     |     R E A D   A G A I N ,   S L O W E R

Read once for meaning, once for grammar. If every part looks fine, run the S-T-A-P-P-A-C checklist one letter at a time.

3.3  Mixed practice: which part has the error?

1.  Each of the players / have / been given / a medal. (a, b, c, d)

2.  He did not / came / to school / yesterday.

3.  She is / one of the / best player / in the team.

4.  I have been / living here / since / five years.

5.  The news / were / shocking / to all.

6.  If it / will rain / we shall / stay home.

7.  He has / a few / knowledge / of Hindi.

8.  Neither Ram / nor Shyam / are / coming.

9.  She prefers / coffee / than / tea.

10.  Mathematics / is / a / interesting subject.

Answers: 1 b (has), 2 b (come), 3 c (best players), 4 c (for), 5 b (was), 6 b (rains), 7 b (little), 8 c (is), 9 c (to), 10 c (an).

6 0 - S E C O N D   R E C A P

Check in order: S-V, tense, article, preposition, pronoun, adjective / adverb, conjunction.

 Signal words point to the rule: since, each, hardly, unless.

 Small words (a / an, to / for) hide most errors.

 No error only when every check passes.

 Read twice: once for sense, once for grammar.
